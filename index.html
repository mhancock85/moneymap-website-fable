03 Meet Marcia

C.P.F.E.® Certified

Hi, I’m Marcia.

I’ve lived, worked and studied in Brazil, Australia and the UK. Each move meant starting from zero: new bank accounts, no credit history, buying everything again. I know how much planning that takes.

After more than 20 years in corporate finance, I moved into personal finance because I wanted my work to help people directly.

I’ve faced big money moments of my own. Finding my first job in a new country was hard, and so was going through a divorce while responsible for myself and my daughter. Because I’ve always been organised with money, I had choices and never felt trapped.

Managing money has always come naturally to me, and it’s what let me study and work abroad. I’d like you to have that same sense of freedom. I know how stressful big changes can be, so I lead every session with empathy.

Today I work with clients around the world. I’m trained in special needs financial planning, so I can support neurodivergent and special needs families. I also help Brazilians living abroad.

Everyone’s experience with money is different and personal, so we start with yours. We’ll get a clear picture of your money and work through any worries or negative feelings that hold you back. Then we’ll build a plan that fits your life.

Family and friends are my anchor. I’m a mum and stepmum to teenagers. I love music, and I play a few instruments and sing with UK Show Choir at some epic venues.

  • Certified Personal Finance Expert (C.P.F.E.®)
  • Member of the Personal Finance Society (UK) and ABEFIN (Brazil)
  • Sessions in English, Portuguese or Spanish
  • Ask me anything. No question is silly.
  • Mental Health First Aider (MHFA England)
